You can order custom merchandise for a society, club, band or charity from a single piece, because there is no minimum order. Start with one item to check the design, then order the quantity your members, fans or supporters will actually buy.

## Why no minimum matters for small groups

Many groups avoid merchandise because of the risk of unsold stock. A student society does not know how many freshers will sign up. A band does not know how many people will turn up to a gig. A charity does not want to spend donations on boxes that sit in a cupboard.

With no minimum order and printing included in the unit price, you can start small. There is no setup fee on these products, so ordering ten does not carry a hidden cost that ordering a hundred would spread out. Prices do fall at higher quantities, so you can scale up once demand is proven.

## Which products suit which group

### Student societies and university clubs

Hoodies and T-shirts are the classic choice, but remember that not everyone wants clothing. Tote bags, bottles and mugs suit freshers' fairs and committee thank-yous. Explore [Men's Clothing](/products?category=c-9225cc69) and [Bags](/products?category=c-a56b2fcd).

### Sports clubs and community teams

Think about what members use at every session. A bottle, a bag, a cap or a towel gets seen constantly. See [Sports & Leisure](/products?category=c-4fb9201e) and [Hats & Accessories](/products?category=c-d78bfb69).

### Bands and musicians

Merch tables at gigs sell small, giftable items. A tote bag with the album art, a mug or a cushion sells to fans who already own the T-shirt. Because most products are printed all-over in full colour, artwork with gradients and photography works well.

### Charities and fundraisers

Branded items can thank volunteers, reward regular donors or be sold at events. Keep the design clean, with the charity name and logo clear. Check the artwork guide in [the guide](/guide) before you upload.

## Planning quantities without wasting money

Follow this simple method:

1. **Order one of each design.** Check the colours, the fit and the print position.
2. **Ask for pre-orders.** Share the mockup preview with your members or fans and take names before you commit.
3. **Order what has been requested, plus a small extra.** Do not guess high.
4. **Reorder when stock runs low.** Orders can be repeated at any time.

Price tiers are shown on each product page, for example 1+, 5+, 15+, 50+, 100+ and 300+. If you are close to the next tier, compare the unit prices before you decide.

## Designing merchandise that people want

- **Keep it simple.** One strong idea beats five small ones.
- **Think about where it will be seen.** A back print on a hoodie is seen by everyone behind the wearer in a queue.
- **Use a transparent PNG or vector logo.** This gives clean edges.
- **Use 300 dpi artwork.** The minimum is 150 dpi, and the designer warns you if the image is too low.
- **Design each side separately.** The front and back of a product can carry different artwork.
- **Remember that colour does not change the price.** You can use as many colours as you want.

Screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result. If your group has a very specific colour, order one piece first and compare.

## Timing for events, gigs and the academic year

Production is commonly around five working days after payment, and the exact time is shown on each product page. Delivery to UK addresses takes about 8–14 working days after dispatch. Delivery is a flat £4.99 per order and free on orders over £50.

Work backwards from your date:

- **Freshers' week:** order over the summer rather than in September.
- **Tours and festivals:** order at least five to six weeks before the first date.
- **Charity events and Christmas fairs:** order early enough to leave time for a reorder if the first batch sells out.

No specific delivery date is promised, so build in a buffer.

## What to know about ordering

Orders are paid by card through Stripe checkout and shipped to UK addresses. Each order goes to one address, so if committee members live in different cities, send it to one person to share out. Items are made to order, so check sizes carefully; there are no returns for change of mind, but faulty or misprinted items are replaced or refunded. See the [returns policy](/returns).

## FAQ

### Is there really no minimum order for custom merchandise?
Yes. Every product can be ordered from one piece, with printing included in the unit price and no setup fee on these products.

### Can a society or charity order a small batch?
Yes. You can order any quantity from one upwards, and the unit price drops at higher tiers shown on each product page.

### How long does delivery take?
Production is commonly around five working days after payment, then delivery takes about 8–14 working days after dispatch. Delivery is £4.99 per order, or free over £50.

### Can we sell the merchandise we order?
Yes, you can sell what you buy to fundraise or cover costs. Set your price above the cost per item including delivery.

### Can I send different items to different addresses in one order?
No. Each order goes to one address, so have it delivered to one person who can distribute the items.
