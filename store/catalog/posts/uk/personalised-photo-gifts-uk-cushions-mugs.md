## Photo quality tips

The photograph decides how the gift looks. Spend a few minutes on it.

- **Use the original file.** A picture sent through a messaging app is usually compressed, so find the original on your phone or camera.
- **Aim for 300 dpi.** This is recommended for the sharpest print, and 150 dpi is the minimum. The designer warns you if resolution is low.
- **Choose a clear subject.** Faces should be well lit and in focus. Blurry or dark photos look worse when enlarged.
- **Leave room around faces.** If you scale or crop, check that no one's head is cut off at an edge.
- **Avoid heavy filters.** Strong filters and tiny text on the picture can look muddy in print.
- **Check the preview.** The live mockup shows the product from several angles before you order.

Screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result, so a photo that is very dark on screen will be darker still on fabric.

## Gifts for UK occasions

### Birthdays

Pick a photo from a shared memory rather than a posed one. For milestone birthdays, a cushion or a mug with a young photo and a recent one side by side makes people smile.

### Weddings and anniversaries

A blanket or cushion with a wedding photo makes a lasting gift for the couple or their parents. Add the date as text below the picture.

### Mother's Day and Father's Day

In the UK, Mother's Day falls in spring and Father's Day in June. Both dates arrive faster than people expect, so order early. A mug with the children's photo or a cushion with a family picture is a dependable choice.

### Christmas

Photo gifts are popular with relatives who live far away, such as grandparents. Order well ahead of December. See [Holiday & Seasonal](/products?category=c-eaba8078) for festive options.

## How to make a photo gift: step by step

This list can be used as written:

1. Choose the product: cushion, blanket or mug.
2. Find the original, highest-resolution version of your photo.

4. Add text such as a name, date or short message if you want one.
5. Check the preview from every angle, and design each print side separately if the product has more than one.
6. Choose your quantity, starting from one, and pay by card.

## FAQ

### What size should my photo be for a cushion or blanket?
Aim for 300 dpi at the print size, and treat 150 dpi as the minimum. The designer warns you if the image resolution is too low.

### Can I put several photos on one gift?
Yes. You can upload more than one image and arrange them in the designer, although each photo will appear smaller. For a collage, choose a blanket, which has more space.

### Can I order just one personalised gift?
Yes. There is no minimum order, so you can order one cushion, blanket or mug. Printing is included in the unit price.

### How early should I order for Mother's Day or Father's Day?
Order at least a month ahead. Production is commonly around five working days and delivery takes about 8–14 working days after dispatch.
