## Why totes work for small businesses

A tote is useful, cheap to carry and visible. A customer who takes one home advertises your stall or shop every time it goes to the supermarket. For independent shops, a printed bag also replaces a plastic or paper carrier with something customers reuse.

## Choosing a size

Think about what goes in the bag, not just how it looks.

- **Small and medium totes** suit cards, stationery, cosmetics, candles and baked goods.
- **Large totes** suit books, clothing, groceries and market produce.
- **Pouches** work for small accessories, jewellery or as a free add-in with a larger purchase.

Check the dimensions on each product page before ordering. If your typical sale is a book or a folded jumper, a larger tote is the safer choice. If you sell small items, a smaller bag looks more generous and costs less per piece.

## Print area and artwork

Every product page shows the printable area in the online designer. A few practical tips:

1. **Keep the logo inside the safe area.** Leave a margin so nothing sits too close to a seam or edge.
2. **Use a transparent PNG or vector logo.** This avoids a white box around your design.
3. **Aim for 300 dpi.** The minimum is 150 dpi, and the designer warns you when resolution is low.
4. **Design each side separately.** The front can carry your logo and the back a website, Instagram handle or a short line of text.
5. **Do not shrink fine detail.** Thin lines and tiny text can disappear at bag scale, so enlarge them or simplify.

Because most items are printed all-over in full colour, you can use a background colour, a pattern or a photograph. The number of colours does not change the price.

## Ordering a small run

There is no minimum order, so you can start with one. Printing is included in the unit price and there is no setup fee on these products.

A sensible approach for a stall or shop:

- **Order one first.** Check the colours, the size and how the logo sits.
- **Then order a first run.** Pick a quantity you expect to give or sell in a couple of months.
- **Reorder when needed.** Prices fall at higher quantity tiers, so a bigger reorder later usually gives a lower unit price.

The quantity tiers are shown on each product page, for example 1+, 5+, 15+, 50+ and 100+. If your sales are steady, look at the next tier up before you check out, because the saving may justify a slightly larger order.

## How tote bags can earn their keep

### Sell them

A branded tote can be a small product in its own right at a market or craft fair. Set your selling price above your cost per bag including delivery.

### Give them away

Use a tote as a free gift with orders over a certain value, or as a thank-you to loyal customers.

### Use them for events

If you run a workshop, pop-up or launch, a tote works as both a goody bag and a keepsake.

## Delivery and timing

Production is shown on each product page and is commonly around five working days, starting after payment. Delivery to UK addresses then takes about 8–14 working days after dispatch. Delivery is a flat £12 per order, and free on orders over £120.

If you have a date to hit, such as a summer fair, a Christmas market or a shop opening, work backwards from it and add a buffer. No specific delivery date is promised, so do not leave it to the last fortnight. If you need other items to go with the bags, put them in the same order so you pay delivery once.

Start with one to check quality, then order what you expect to sell or give away in the next couple of months. Higher quantity tiers lower the unit price, so reorder in larger batches once you know demand.

### Can I print different designs on the front and back?
Yes. Each print side can be designed separately, so you can put a logo on one side and contact details on the other.
