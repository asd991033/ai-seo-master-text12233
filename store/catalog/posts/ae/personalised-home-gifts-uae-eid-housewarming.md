Personalised home textiles such as cushions, blankets, towels and tablecloths make thoughtful gifts for UAE housewarmings, Eid gatherings and family occasions, because they are useful, lasting and personal. Choose a design that suits the recipient's home, keep it tasteful, and order early since each piece is made to order and shipped from overseas.

## Why home gifts work well in the UAE

Family and hospitality are central to life in the UAE. Gatherings at home are frequent, from Eid visits to family dinners and welcoming new neighbours. A gift for the home is a natural, respectful way to take part. Textiles are especially suitable: they are practical, easy to store and they bring colour to a room.

The [Home & Living category](/products?category=c-229a0246) covers blankets, cushions, tablecloths, towels and kitchen textiles.

## Choosing the right product

### Cushions

Cushions are a simple, affordable way to add personality to a living room or majlis. A single design can reflect the recipient's taste, a family name or a favourite photograph. They are an easy choice when you are unsure of the recipient's style.

### Blankets

Blankets are a comfortable, generous gift. In the UAE, air conditioning makes a soft throw a daily comfort, indoors and on cooler evenings in winter. A blanket with a family photograph or a calm pattern suits new homes and family milestones.

### Towels

Towels are practical housewarming gifts. A set with a monogram or a pattern feels considered and will be used for years.

### Tablecloths

Tablecloths suit hosts. For Eid lunches and family dinners, a tablecloth in festive or elegant colours helps the table look special. You can [search for tablecloth designs](/products?q=tablecloth) to see the options.

## Designing a personal gift

Most products are printed all-over in full colour by transfer or dye-sublimation, so photographs and gradients work well. That gives you plenty of creative freedom.

- **Use a favourite photo:** a family portrait, a view or a child's drawing can become a keepsake. Choose a sharp image, 300 dpi if possible and 150 dpi at minimum.
- **Add text with care:** a family name, a short blessing or a simple greeting is warm and appropriate. Keep wording respectful and easy to read.
- **Choose colours that fit the room:** soft neutrals suit most homes, while richer tones feel festive.
- **Check the mockup:** the live preview shows the product from several angles, and each print side can be designed separately.

Remember that screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result.

## Occasions and what to give

- **Housewarming:** a cushion or a set of towels, personalised with a family name or a simple pattern.
- **Eid:** a tablecloth or blanket for a host, or a cushion for relatives, with a warm greeting. Choose designs that are festive but tasteful.
- **Weddings and new babies:** a blanket with a name or date makes a lasting keepsake.
- **Birthdays and anniversaries:** a photo cushion or blanket brings memories into the home.
- **Thank-you gifts:** a smaller item such as a kitchen textile is a modest, kind gesture.

## FAQ

### What is a good housewarming gift for a UAE home?
A personalised cushion, blanket or set of towels is a safe, useful choice. Add a family name or a calm pattern for a personal touch.

### Can I print a family photo on a blanket or cushion?
Yes. Upload a clear image, ideally 300 dpi, and check the live mockup before ordering. The designer warns you if the resolution is too low.

### How early should I order for Eid?
Order as early as you can. Allow around 5 working days for production and about 8 to 14 working days for delivery after dispatch, plus a buffer.

### Can I send gifts to several relatives in one order?
No, each order ships to a single address. You can place separate orders for different addresses, or receive the gifts yourself and pass them on.

### Can I return a personalised item if the recipient does not like it?
Items are made to order, so returns for change of mind are not accepted. Faulty, misprinted, wrong or transit-damaged items are remade or refunded if reported within 7 days with photos. See the [returns policy](/returns).
