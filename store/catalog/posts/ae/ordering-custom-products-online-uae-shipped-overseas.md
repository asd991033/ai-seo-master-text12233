Ordering custom products online from the UAE is a simple sequence: design your product in the browser, check the live mockup, pay by card, wait for production, and receive it by delivery to your UAE address. Because the order ships from overseas, allow production time plus about 8 to 14 working days for delivery after dispatch.

## The process in plain steps

Here is the whole journey, from idea to delivery:

1. **Choose a product** from the range of about 990 custom products.
2. **Design online** by uploading a logo or image, or by adding text.
3. **Check the live mockup** from several angles.
4. **Choose quantity** and see the unit price for that tier.
5. **Pay by card** through Stripe checkout, in UAE dirhams (AED).
6. **Production** begins after payment.
7. **Dispatch and delivery** to your UAE address.

You can read more in the [ordering guide](/guide).

## Step 1: Design in your browser

You do not need design software. Open a product, upload your artwork or type text, then drag, scale and rotate it into position. Each print side can be designed separately, so a bag or a mug can carry a different design on each side.

### Artwork that prints well

- Use 300 dpi where possible; 150 dpi is the minimum.
- Use a transparent PNG or a vector file for logos.
- Watch for the warning in the designer if resolution is too low.

Most products are printed all-over and in full colour by transfer or dye-sublimation, so photographs and gradients are fine and the number of colours does not change the price.

## Step 2: Proof with the live mockup

Instead of waiting for a proof by email, you see a live mockup of the product from several angles while you design. This is your main proof, so take your time. Check spelling, placement, and how the design wraps around curved items such as drinkware. Remember that screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result.

If you want to see the physical product before a large order, order 1 piece first. There is no minimum order, and that is the way to hold a finished item in your hands.

## Step 3: Quantity, price and payment

Printing is included in the unit price and there is no setup fee. The unit price falls as you order more, with price tiers shown on each product page. Prices are in UAE dirhams.

Delivery is a flat AED 55 per order, and it is free on orders over AED 550. Payment is by card through Stripe checkout. If import duties or taxes are charged by local customs, they are paid by the recipient.

## Step 4: Production and delivery times

Production starts after payment and the time is shown on each product page, commonly around 5 working days. When the order is dispatched, delivery takes about 8 to 14 working days to reach a UAE address.

These are estimates, not promises of a specific date. Allow a safe margin, especially for dated events such as Ramadan, Eid, National Day or an exhibition. As a rough guide, add production time and delivery time together, then add extra days for checking artwork and for anything unexpected.

Each order is delivered to a single address. If you need items in several places, place separate orders or share them out yourself after delivery.

## If you change your mind or something goes wrong

### Cancelling

You can cancel an order for a full refund before production starts. After that, the item is being made specially for you.

### Faulty or damaged items

Because items are made to order, there are no returns for change of mind. However, faulty, misprinted, wrong or transit-damaged items are remade or refunded free of charge if you report them within 7 days of delivery with photos. Photograph the item and its packaging as soon as it arrives. See the full [returns policy](/returns).

## Tips for ordering from overseas with confidence

- Start early and keep a buffer for delivery.
- Check the mockup on every angle before paying.
- Use clear, high-resolution artwork.
- Order one piece first if the project is important.
- Keep delivery details accurate, including the building and area, so the order arrives smoothly.
