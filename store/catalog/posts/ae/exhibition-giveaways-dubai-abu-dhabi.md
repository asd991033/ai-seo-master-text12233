The best exhibition giveaways for Dubai and Abu Dhabi are practical items visitors will carry and use again: custom bags, drinkware and tech accessories. Pick one or two products that suit your audience, keep the branding clear but uncluttered, and order early, because custom products are made to order and shipped from overseas.

## Start with what visitors will actually keep

Trade halls are crowded and visitors collect a lot. The giveaway that survives the trip home is the one that is useful in daily life. Ask yourself whether someone would use it at work, in the car or at home.

Good giveaways share three qualities:

- **Useful:** it solves a small everyday need.
- **Visible:** your logo is seen each time it is used.
- **Portable:** easy to carry through a hall, a hotel or an airport.

## Bags that do the walking for you

A bag is one of the most effective event items because people use it during the event itself. Your branding then moves around the venue.

### Totes and pouches

[Totes and pouches](/products?category=c-a56b2fcd) are lightweight and easy to hand out. A tote can carry brochures from other stands, so visitors tend to use it straight away. Because most products are printed all-over in full colour, gradients and photographs reproduce well.

### Backpacks

Backpacks suit conferences with delegates who carry laptops and documents. They are a more considered gift, so they often work better for registered delegates or VIP guests than for walk-in visitors.

## Drinkware for a hot climate

In the UAE, staying hydrated is a daily habit, so [mugs, tumblers and bottles](/products?category=c-51baa713) are a natural fit. A reusable bottle or tumbler with your logo will be seen in offices, gyms and cars. Keep the design clean so it still looks professional months later.

## Tech accessories people keep on their desk

[Mouse pads and desk mats](/products?category=c-169b68e3) live on a desk and are seen for years. They are well suited to technology, finance, logistics and professional-services events where your audience works at a computer all day. A small QR-style design element or a short web address can invite follow-up, provided the artwork is sharp and high resolution.

## Match the giveaway to the audience

Not every attendee needs the same item. Consider tiers:

- **Walk-in visitors:** a lightweight item such as a pouch or tote.
- **Qualified leads:** a practical item such as a bottle or desk mat, given after a conversation at your stand.
- **VIPs and speakers:** a higher-quality item, ideally personalised.

This keeps your budget focused where it counts, and lets you start conversations rather than just handing items out.

## Quick checklist for event giveaways

Use this list to organise an exhibition giveaway order:

1. Confirm the event date and the number of expected visitors.
2. Choose one or two practical products.
3. Set a budget per piece and per visitor tier.
4. Prepare artwork as a transparent PNG or vector file, 300 dpi preferred, 150 dpi minimum.
5. Design online and review every side in the mockup.
6. Order early to allow for production and delivery.
7. Receive the order at one address and arrange transport to the venue.

## Design tips that help at events

- Keep one clear message: logo, company name and a simple web address.
- Use strong contrast so the logo reads from a distance.
- Check that colours look right on the product, remembering that screens can differ slightly from the printed result.
- Use each print side purposefully, for example a logo on one side and an event name on the other.
