## Why drinkware suits the UAE

Drinkware is one of the most practical branded products in a hot climate. People carry water between air-conditioned buildings and the outdoors, keep a bottle in the car and a mug on the desk. A product used every day keeps your logo visible, which is why mugs, tumblers and bottles are popular for staff, clients and events.

## Mugs, tumblers or bottles: which to choose

Each type has a different job. Matching the product to the use is the most important decision.

### Mugs

Mugs suit desks, pantries and meeting rooms. They are a good everyday gift for office staff and welcome packs for new joiners. Since a mug is often used for tea and coffee, a calm, elegant design tends to feel more professional than a busy one.

### Tumblers

Tumblers are easy to carry, which makes them ideal for people who commute or move between sites. They suit cold drinks as well as hot ones, and a printed wrap lets you use the full surface for a design.

### Water bottles

Bottles are the practical choice for hydration throughout the day, in the gym, in the car or on site visits. They are especially useful for teams who work outdoors or travel between locations, such as logistics, construction and events.

## Designing drinkware that looks good

Most products are printed all-over in full colour by transfer or dye-sublimation, so gradients and photographs are fine and the number of colours does not change the price. A few simple choices make a big difference.

- **Keep the logo clear:** use a transparent PNG or a vector file so edges stay crisp.
- **Use good resolution:** 300 dpi is recommended and 150 dpi is the minimum. The designer warns you if resolution is low.
- **Think about the curve:** a design wraps around a cylinder, so keep key elements away from the very edges and check every angle in the live mockup.
- **Choose contrast:** a light logo on a dark surface, or the reverse, reads well from across a room.
- **Use each side:** print sides can be designed separately, for example a logo on one and a name or short message on the other.

## FAQ

### Which is better for an office: a mug, tumbler or bottle?
It depends on use. Mugs suit desks and meeting rooms, tumblers suit commuting and cold drinks, and bottles suit all-day hydration. Many offices offer a bottle and a mug together.

### Can I put a photo or gradient on drinkware?
Yes. Most products are printed all-over in full colour, so photos and gradients are fine and do not cost more than a single-colour logo.

### Can I order just one custom mug?
Yes. Every product can be ordered from 1 piece, and the unit price drops as you order more.

### How long does a custom drinkware order take to reach the UAE?
Production commonly takes around 5 working days after payment, and delivery takes about 8 to 14 working days after dispatch. Order early for dated events.

### What if my order arrives broken?
Report it within 7 days of delivery with photos. Faulty, misprinted, wrong or transit-damaged items are remade or refunded free of charge. See the [returns policy](/returns).
