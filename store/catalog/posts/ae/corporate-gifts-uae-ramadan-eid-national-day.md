The best corporate gifts in the UAE are useful, tasteful and timed to the occasion: Ramadan and Eid greetings, UAE National Day, client appreciation and year-end thank-yous. Choose everyday items such as drinkware, bags or tech accessories carrying a subtle logo, set a clear budget per recipient, and order several weeks ahead because custom products are made to order and shipped from overseas.

## Know the gifting calendar

Corporate gifting in the UAE follows a few clear moments. Each one has its own tone, so it helps to decide early which ones your company will mark.

- **Ramadan and Eid Al Fitr:** a time of generosity and goodwill. Gifts are usually given in the days leading up to Eid, or as a thoughtful gesture to colleagues and partners during the month.
- **Eid Al Adha:** another occasion for warm greetings, often with a smaller gesture than Eid Al Fitr.
- **UAE National Day (2 December):** a celebration many companies join with staff and client gifts in national colours.
- **Year-end and new year:** a natural time for client appreciation and team thank-yous.
- **Ongoing moments:** new client onboarding, project completion, work anniversaries and staff milestones.

Dates for Ramadan and the Eids move each year with the lunar calendar, so check the confirmed dates before you set your order deadline.

## Choose gifts that suit the occasion

Respect and relevance matter more than size. Keep designs elegant and avoid imagery or items that could be inappropriate for a diverse, largely Muslim workforce and client base.

### Safe, appreciated categories

- **Drinkware:** [mugs, tumblers and bottles](/products?category=c-51baa713) are used daily in offices and suit the climate.
- **Bags:** [totes, backpacks and pouches](/products?category=c-a56b2fcd) work for events and everyday carrying.
- **Tech accessories:** [mouse pads and desk mats](/products?category=c-169b68e3) sit on a desk and keep your brand visible.
- **Home textiles:** cushions, blankets and towels suit family-oriented occasions such as Eid.

### What to avoid

- Items connected with alcohol or anything that could cause offence.
- Overly loud branding. A discreet logo or a seasonal greeting feels more respectful than a large advert.
- Anything that cannot be used practically. People keep what they use.

## Set a budget per head

Decide the budget before you browse. The ranges below are planning guidelines for your own budgeting, not product prices.

- **Teams and staff:** a modest, uniform gift for everyone feels fair.
- **Regular clients:** a step up in quality or a small set of two items.
- **Key clients and senior contacts:** a more considered gift, perhaps personalised with a name or a greeting.

Remember delivery: it is a flat AED 32 per order and free on orders over AED 300, so consolidating a team order into one shipment often makes sense. Unit prices also fall as quantities rise, with price tiers shown on each product page, so ordering for the whole group at once is usually more economical than many small orders.

## How to plan your timeline

Working backwards is the safest approach. A custom order has three stages: design, production and delivery.

1. **Design:** upload your logo or greeting, position it and check the live mockup from several angles.
2. **Production:** shown on each product page, commonly around 5 working days after payment.
3. **Delivery:** about 8 to 14 working days after dispatch, to a UAE address.

Add time for internal approval and for you to hand the gifts out. For Ramadan, Eid or National Day, place the order early rather than aiming for a specific date, because delivery times are estimates and not guarantees.

## A simple checklist for corporate gift orders

Use this short list to plan any corporate gift order:

1. Choose the occasion and confirm the date.
2. Set a budget per recipient and the total quantity.
3. Pick one or two product types that are practical and suitable.
4. Prepare artwork: a transparent PNG or vector logo, ideally 300 dpi (150 dpi minimum).
5. Design online and review the mockup on every side.
6. Order early, allowing for production and delivery.
7. Distribute the gifts yourself, as each order ships to a single address.

## Practical tips for a good result

- Order one piece first if the gift is important. There is no minimum order, so you can check the real product before placing a larger one.
- Keep colours consistent with your brand, remembering that screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result.
- Print each side separately where useful, for example a logo on one side and a short greeting on the other.
- Keep the message simple. A short seasonal phrase in English, with your company name, is enough.

## FAQ

### When should I order corporate gifts for Ramadan or Eid?
Order as early as you can. Allow around 5 working days for production plus about 8 to 14 working days for delivery after dispatch, and extra time for approvals and handing gifts out.

### Can I order small quantities for key clients?
Yes. Every product can be ordered from 1 piece, so you can personalise a handful of gifts for key contacts and order larger quantities for staff.

### Can you deliver to several offices or recipients in one order?
Each order goes to one UAE address. If you need gifts at several locations, place a separate order for each, or receive them in one place and distribute them yourself.

### How much does delivery cost?
Delivery is a flat AED 32 per order, and it is free on orders over AED 300. Payment is by card through Stripe checkout.

### What if a gift arrives faulty or misprinted?
Report it within 7 days of delivery with photos. Faulty, misprinted, wrong or transit-damaged items are remade or refunded free of charge, as set out in the [returns policy](/returns).
