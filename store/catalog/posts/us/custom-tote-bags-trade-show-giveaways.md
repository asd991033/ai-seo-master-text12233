A custom tote bag is one of the giveaways attendees are most likely to keep, because they can use it right away to carry everything else they collect. To get it right, pick one useful item, estimate quantities from your booth goals, and order early enough to cover production and delivery.

## What attendees actually keep

At a busy conference, people leave with a pile of paper and small items. Most of it ends up in a hotel wastebasket. The things that survive the trip home share a few traits:

- They are useful on the show floor itself.
- They look good enough to use in everyday life.
- They are easy to pack.

A tote bag meets all three. Attendees fill it at your booth and carry it for the rest of the event, so your logo travels through the aisles for you.

## Design for a crowded hall

Your bag will be seen from several feet away, so simple wins.

1. **One clear message.** Your logo, plus a short line or your booth number.
2. **Strong contrast.** Dark artwork on a light bag or the reverse.
3. **Both sides in play.** Each print side can be designed separately, so use one for your brand and one for a pattern or an event name.
4. **A reason to keep it.** An attractive illustration will outlast a plain logo.

Most products are printed all-over in full color, which means gradients and photos are fine. Color count does not change the price, so you can use your full brand palette.

Upload a transparent PNG or a vector logo, ideally at 300 dpi. The designer warns you if resolution is low. Our [artwork guidelines](/guide) explain the details.

## Planning quantities

There is no perfect formula, but you can reason it out in a few steps.

1. **Define the purpose.** Are you handing a tote to everyone who stops, or only to qualified leads?
2. **Estimate conversations, not attendance.** Total event attendance is much larger than the number of people who reach your booth.
3. **Decide who gets what.** One group might receive a bag, another a smaller item.
4. **Add a small buffer** for team members, partners and surprises.

Since there is no minimum order, you can start with a modest quantity and reorder if the show goes well. Unit prices drop at tiers shown on each product page, such as 50+, 100+ and 300+, so a slightly larger order may cost less per piece. Compare the tiers before you finalize.

If you are unsure about the design, order 1 piece first. It is the only sample option, but it shows you the real print, size and feel.

## Timing: work backward from the show

Dated events leave no room for delays, so build your schedule from the opening day.

- **Production:** commonly around 5 working days after payment, as shown on each product page.
- **Delivery:** about 8 to 14 working days after dispatch within the United States.
- **Buffer:** at least a week or two for design tweaks, a remake or slow carrier days.

Add those up and you will see that you need to order several weeks before the show. Delivery is not promised for a specific date, so do not wait until the last minute. Ship to the office or the venue address that will actually be staffed when the package arrives.

## Quick checklist: ordering trade show giveaways

**Trade show giveaway checklist** (a short version you can reuse):

1. Pick one useful item, ideally a tote bag.
2. Prepare your logo as a transparent PNG or vector file.
3. Design both print sides in the online designer.
4. Check the mockup from every angle.
5. Order 1 piece to confirm quality.
6. Place the full order at the right price tier.
7. Allow production, delivery and a buffer before show day.

## FAQ

### How many tote bags should I order for a trade show?

Base it on the number of booth conversations you expect, not total attendance. Add a small buffer for staff and partners. You can start small and reorder, since there is no minimum.

### How early should I order giveaways for a conference?

Order several weeks ahead. Production commonly takes around 5 working days, and delivery takes about 8 to 14 working days after dispatch. Leave extra time for corrections.

### Can I print different designs on each side of a tote?

Yes. Each print side can be designed separately in the online designer, and you can preview the mockup from several angles.

### Do I have to pay shipping on a large booth order?

Delivery is a flat $15 per order and free on orders over $150. Large giveaway orders often qualify for free delivery.

### What if my order arrives misprinted?

Report it within 7 days of delivery with photos. Faulty, misprinted, wrong or damaged items are remade or refunded free of charge.
