The best custom corporate gifts for a small business are useful, simple to personalize and ordered in the quantity you actually need. With no minimum order, you can order one piece to check the quality, then place the full order once you are happy with how it looks.

This guide covers the three moments when small teams give gifts most often: employee appreciation, onboarding and the holiday season.

## Why small teams do gifting differently

A ten-person company has different needs than a large corporation. You probably do not have a procurement department or a storage room full of boxes. You want a gift that feels personal and arrives without a lot of coordination.

Because every product on Kindmark can be ordered from 1 piece, you are not forced to guess headcount months ahead. Unit prices drop as quantities rise, so you can see how much a slightly bigger order saves before you commit.

## Employee appreciation gifts that get used

A gift works when people reach for it again after the first week. Think about what your team does all day.

### Match the gift to the work

- **Office and hybrid teams:** mugs, tumblers and water bottles from the [Mugs, Tumblers & Bottles](/products?category=c-51baa713) category, or desk mats and mouse pads from the Tech Accessories category.
- **Field and shop teams:** [caps and accessories](/products?category=c-d78bfb69) or a sturdy tote from [Bags](/products?category=c-a56b2fcd).
- **Remote teams:** soft home items such as blankets or cushions from the Home & Living category, which feel more like a thank-you than a logo giveaway.

### Keep the branding light

A small logo or a short line of text usually looks better than a design that covers everything. Because most products are printed all-over in full color, you can also go bold with a pattern in your brand colors. Either approach works, so choose the one that fits your company's personality.

## Onboarding kits for new hires

A welcome kit tells a new employee that you were expecting them. Keep it to two or three items so it feels thoughtful rather than crowded.

1. **A drinkware piece** that they will use at their desk on day one.
2. **A bag or pouch** for carrying a laptop, notebooks or lunch.
3. **One personal touch,** such as the new hire's first name added through the online designer.

Since orders can start at a single piece, you can place a small order every time someone joins instead of stocking up. Use the same design each time so the kits stay consistent.

One thing to plan for: each order ships to a single delivery address. If your team is spread across the country, send the order to one person or office, who can pass the items on.

## Holiday gifting season

Holiday orders are where timing matters most. Production usually takes around 5 working days after payment, which is shown on each product page. After dispatch, delivery to a United States address takes about 8 to 14 working days.

That means a December gift needs to be ordered well before the month starts. Work backward from the date you want items in hand, add production and delivery time, then add a cushion for design changes.

The [Holiday & Seasonal](/products?category=c-eaba8078) category is a good place to begin if you want something festive.

## Quick definition: how to order custom corporate gifts

**Custom corporate gifts** are branded items you give to employees, clients or partners. To order them from a print-on-demand store:

1. Choose a product and open its page.
2. Upload your logo or add text in the online designer.
3. Check the live mockup from several angles.
4. Order 1 piece first to confirm quality.
5. Reorder in the quantity you need, using the price tiers on the product page.
6. Pay by card at checkout and wait for dispatch.

### Mistakes to avoid

- **Ordering everything at once.** Test one piece first.
- **Low-resolution logos.** Upload a transparent PNG or vector file, and aim for 300 dpi.
- **Waiting until the last week.** Dated events leave no room for remakes.
- **Skipping the mockup check.** Look at every angle before paying.

Items are made to order, so there are no returns for change of mind. If something arrives faulty, misprinted or damaged, report it within 7 days of delivery with photos and it will be remade or refunded free of charge. See the [returns policy](/returns) for details.

## FAQ

### Is there a minimum order for custom corporate gifts?

No. Every product can be ordered from 1 piece. The unit price drops at higher quantities, and the tiers are shown on each product page.

### How long does delivery take in the United States?

Production commonly takes around 5 working days after payment. Delivery then takes about 8 to 14 working days after dispatch. Order early for dated events.

### How much does shipping cost?

Delivery is a flat $15 per order and free on orders over $150.

### Can I ship gifts to several employees from one order?

Each order goes to a single address. To reach several people, send the order to one contact who can distribute the items.

### Can I see my gift before ordering a full batch?

You can preview a live mockup from several angles in the designer. For a physical check, order 1 piece first, then place your full order.
