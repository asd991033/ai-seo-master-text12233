No-minimum custom merch lets you test a design with a single piece, fix what you see, and only then order more. Start with 1 piece to check print quality and fit, then scale up through price tiers as friends, fans or members ask for more.

## Why ordering one piece first makes sense

Traditional merch orders ask you to commit to dozens of pieces before you know whether anyone wants them. That is a risk for a creator with a new logo, a small brand with a tight budget or a club still counting members.

Ordering 1 piece first changes the math:

- **You see the real print.** Screen colors can differ slightly from the printed result, and a physical piece settles the question.
- **You check the size and feel.** A design that looks right on screen may need to be larger, smaller or moved.
- **You avoid dead stock.** Nothing sits in a closet unsold.
- **You can share it.** A photo of the finished piece is great for pre-orders or a club vote.

Ordering one piece is also the only sample option, because the store does not offer separate pre-production samples. In practice, that is enough: it is the real product, made the same way as a bigger order.

## Who this works well for

### Creators

Streamers, podcasters, artists and writers can turn a logo, mascot or illustration into [mugs, tumblers and bottles](/products?category=c-51baa713) or apparel. Start with a single item for yourself, post it, and see if your audience asks for it.

### Small brands

A new brand can use merch as a low-cost extension of its identity. Try a [tote](/products?q=tote) or a cap from [Hats & Accessories](/products?category=c-d78bfb69) before expanding the line.

### Clubs, teams and groups

School clubs, sports teams, community groups and hobby circles often have uneven needs. One member wants a hoodie, another a bag. You can order what each person needs rather than forcing a single item on everyone. Sports & Leisure and Kids' Clothing categories have plenty of ideas.

## How price tiers work

Each product page shows price tiers, for example 1+, 5+, 15+, 50+, 100+ and 300+. The more pieces of one product you order, the lower the unit price becomes.

Here is how to think about it:

- **1+ tier:** ideal for testing and one-off gifts.
- **5+ and 15+ tiers:** good for a small team, a podcast crew or a first batch of pre-orders.
- **50+ and above:** useful once you have steady demand.

Printing is included in the unit price, with no setup fee on these products. Because most items are printed all-over in full color, adding more colors or a photo does not change the price.

## A simple scaling plan

**How to scale custom merch from 1 piece (quotable steps):**

1. Design your artwork in the online designer and check the mockup from several angles.
2. Order 1 piece of the product.
3. Inspect the print, size and quality when it arrives.
4. Adjust the design if needed.
5. Order a small batch, such as 5 or 15 pieces, to cover early buyers or members.
6. Move to higher tiers when demand is steady.

This plan keeps your cash flow healthy and lets real feedback guide each decision.

## Preparing your artwork

You do not need a professional designer. You do need clean files.

- Use a **transparent PNG** or **vector** file for logos.
- Aim for **300 dpi**, with 150 dpi as the minimum. The designer warns you when resolution is low.
- Keep text away from edges.

### Common mistakes

- Skipping the 1-piece test and ordering a big batch immediately.
- Uploading a small, blurry image.
- Forgetting that each order goes to one address, so plan how members will receive their items.
- Waiting until the week of the event.

## FAQ

### Is there really no minimum order for custom merch?

Correct. Every product can be ordered from 1 piece, and there is no setup fee on these products.

### Why should I order just one piece first?

It shows you the real print, size and quality before you commit to more. It is also the only way to get a physical sample.

### How does the price drop as I order more?

Each product page lists price tiers, such as 1+, 5+, 15+, 50+, 100+ and 300+. Higher quantities of the same product lower the unit price.

### Can I order different items for different club members?

Yes, you can order different products and quantities. All items in one order ship to a single address, so one person would need to hand them out.

### How long until my merch arrives?

Production commonly takes around 5 working days after payment, then delivery takes about 8 to 14 working days after dispatch.
