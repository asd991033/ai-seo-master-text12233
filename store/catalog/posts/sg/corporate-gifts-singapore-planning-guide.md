The best way to handle corporate gifts in Singapore is to plan backwards from the occasion: pick the festive moment, set a budget per head, and place your order at least four weeks before you need the items in hand. Custom-printed practical items such as mugs, tote bags and desk accessories work across cultures and seasons, which suits Singapore's mixed workforce and client base.

## The Singapore corporate gifting calendar

Singapore's working year has several natural gifting moments. Each has its own tone, so match the item to the occasion rather than reusing one gift all year.

### Chinese New Year (January or February)
This is the busiest gifting period for many companies. Clients and suppliers often expect a token before the festive break. Red and gold colourways feel appropriate, and practical gifts such as [tote bags](/products?category=c-a56b2fcd) or drinkware are easy to give and use.

### Hari Raya Puasa and Hari Raya Haji
Greetings and small tokens for Muslim colleagues and partners are appreciated. Keep designs warm and understated, with greens, golds or soft neutrals, and avoid anything that clashes with dietary or religious sensitivities.

### Deepavali (October or November)
Bright, jewel-toned designs suit Deepavali. As with other festivals, a useful everyday item with a tasteful message is safer than anything heavily symbolic.

### National Day (August)
Red and white designs, with a subtle local motif, work well for staff giveaways and community events. This is also a good moment for team-wide gifts that are not tied to any one culture.

### Year-end and Dinner & Dance season
From November to January, many firms hold annual Dinner & Dance events and send year-end thank-you gifts to clients. Order early, because every other company is doing the same.

## How much to spend per head

Budgets vary by industry, but a simple tiered approach keeps things fair and manageable:

- **Mass staff or event gifts:** a low-cost, useful item with your logo, given to everyone.
- **Regular clients and partners:** a mid-range item, ideally one they will use at their desk.
- **Key accounts and senior contacts:** a more considered item, perhaps a small set, with a personalised message.

Because there is no minimum order, you can mix tiers easily. Order 200 simple pieces for staff and 10 of a different product for key accounts, with no penalty for the smaller batch. Unit prices fall as quantities rise, so check the price tiers shown on each product page before you finalise numbers.

## Lead times: working backwards from the date

Allow for three stages:

1. **Design and approval:** a day or two to finalise artwork and check the live mockup.
2. **Production:** commonly around 5 working days after payment, shown on each product page.
3. **Delivery:** about 7 to 12 working days after dispatch to Singapore addresses.

Add those together and give yourself a buffer. For Chinese New Year, which has a hard deadline before the break, aim to order about a month ahead. Delivery is a flat S$9.90 per order, and free on orders over S$80, so consolidating into one order is usually more economical.

## A quotable checklist for corporate gifting

**How to plan a corporate gift order in Singapore:**

1. Choose the occasion and confirm the date you need the gifts in hand.
2. Count recipients and split them into tiers (staff, clients, key accounts).
3. Set a budget per head for each tier.
4. Pick a practical product type for each tier.
5. Upload your logo and check the mockup from every angle.
6. Order at least four weeks before the date, in a single order to one address.
7. Distribute the gifts yourself once they arrive.

Practical beats flashy. Reusable [mugs, tumblers and bottles](/products?category=c-51baa713) are used daily, and [tech accessories](/products?category=c-169b68e3) such as desk mats suit office workers. For seasonal designs, browse [Holiday & Seasonal](/products?category=c-eaba8078).

A few design tips:

- Use a transparent PNG or vector logo, ideally at 300 dpi, for crisp results.
- Keep the message short and warm.
- Most products are printed full-colour, so festive gradients and patterns are fine.
- Remember that screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result.

## FAQ

### When should I order corporate gifts for Chinese New Year?
Order about a month before you need them. Production is commonly around 5 working days, and delivery takes about 7 to 12 working days after dispatch, so a month leaves a sensible buffer.

### Can I order a small number of corporate gifts?
Yes. Every product can be ordered from 1 piece, so you can send a handful of premium items to key clients alongside a larger staff order.

### Is delivery free for corporate orders?
Delivery is a flat S$9.90 per order and free on orders over S$80. Bigger orders therefore usually qualify.

### Can I send gifts to several recipients directly?
Each order goes to one Singapore address. Most companies receive the full order and distribute items internally.

### Can I see what the gift looks like before paying?
You can preview a live mockup from several angles while you design. If you want to see the physical item first, order a single piece.
