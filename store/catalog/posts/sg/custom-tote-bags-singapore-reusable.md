To choose a custom tote bag in Singapore, decide first what it will carry, then pick a size to match and keep the artwork inside the main print area. A well-sized tote with a simple design gets reused for groceries, work and errands, which is exactly what you want from a branded bag.

## Why totes suit Singapore

Singapore shoppers are used to carrying their own bags, whether for supermarket runs, hawker centre takeaways, library books or the commute. A tote that folds small and holds a surprising amount is easy to keep in a work bag. That daily use is what makes a custom tote a good choice for events, businesses and personal gifts: it travels around town with your design on it.

## Match the size to the job

Rather than guessing, think about the load.

- **Everyday and groceries:** a roomy tote with comfortable handles that can sit on a shoulder.
- **Work and school:** a medium tote that fits a laptop, a water bottle and a notebook.
- **Events and door gifts:** a lighter, flatter tote that holds a few takeaways and packs flat for distribution.
- **Small essentials:** a pouch for cables, make-up or coins.

## Understanding the print area

The print area is the zone where your design will appear. It is shown in the online designer, so you can drag, scale and rotate your artwork and see it on a live mockup from several angles.

### Keep important elements inside the area
Logos and text near the edge may feel cramped or risk looking cut off. Leave a small margin and check the preview.

### Design each side separately
Where the product allows it, each print side can have its own design, such as a logo on one side and a pattern or message on the other.

### Think about how the bag hangs
A bag held on a shoulder shows its side differently from one laid flat. A centred design works best for most uses, and a lower-placed design can look good when the bag is full.

## A quotable guide to designing a custom tote

**How to design a custom tote bag in five steps:**

1. Decide how the bag will be used and choose a suitable size.
2. Upload a logo or image, ideally a transparent PNG or vector file at 300 dpi.
3. Place and scale the artwork inside the print area, leaving a small margin.
4. Check the live mockup from every angle, and heed any low-resolution warning.
5. Choose your quantity, review the price tier and pay by card at checkout.

## Artwork tips that save disappointment

- **Resolution:** 300 dpi is recommended and 150 dpi is the minimum. The designer warns you when an image is too low in resolution.
- **Colours:** most products are printed all-over in full colour, so gradients and photos are fine and colour count does not change the price.
- **Screen versus print:** colours on screen can differ slightly from the printed result.
- **Contrast:** a pale design on a pale bag will fade out, so choose a background that lets your artwork stand out.
