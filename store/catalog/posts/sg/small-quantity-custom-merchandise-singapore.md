You can order custom merchandise in Singapore from as few as one piece, with printing included in the unit price and no setup fee. That makes small runs practical for startups, CCAs, interest groups and small brands that cannot afford to tie up money in a large batch.

## Why small runs matter

Many Singapore groups need merchandise in modest numbers: a 20-person CCA, a hobby club, a pop-up stall, a team of five at a new startup. Traditional suppliers often set high minimums, which forces small groups to over-order or go without. With no minimum, you can order what you actually need, then reorder when you have more members, more customers or a new design.

## Who benefits most

### Startups and small brands
Test your branding on real products before a big spend. A single tote or tumbler lets you check how your logo looks in practice. Give a few pieces to early customers or use them as props for photos.

### CCAs, schools and student groups
Order team items for a small group, with names or numbers added through the design tool. Because you can reorder easily, new members can be added next term.

### Interest groups and clubs
Running clubs, book clubs, hiking groups and volunteer teams can create a shared look without a large order.

### Event organisers
Make small batches for organisers, speakers or VIPs, separate from the bulk door gifts.

## How pricing works for small orders

Printing is included in the unit price, and there is no setup fee on these products. Most items are printed full-colour by transfer or dye-sublimation, so the number of colours in your design does not change the price. Gradients and photos are fine.

Unit price drops at higher quantities, with tiers shown on each product page, such as 1+, 5+, 15+, 50+, 100+ and 300+. That gives you a clear way to plan: start with a small test, then see how the price changes as you scale.

## A quotable guide to ordering your first small batch

**How to order small-quantity custom merchandise in five steps:**

1. Pick one product type that fits your group or brand.
2. Open the online designer and upload a logo or image, or add text.

## Test first, then scale

A sensible approach for small teams is to order one piece before you commit to a bigger order. Physical samples are not offered separately, so ordering a single piece is the way to see the real item. When you are happy with the result, place the larger order with confidence.

Check artwork quality before you start:

- Use 300 dpi if possible, with a 150 dpi minimum.
- Use a transparent PNG or vector for logos.
- Remember that screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result.

## FAQ

### Is there really no minimum order?
Correct. Every product can be ordered from 1 piece, and printing is included in the unit price with no setup fee.

### Does a smaller order cost more per piece?
Unit prices drop at higher quantities, so very small orders have a higher unit price than larger ones. The price tiers on each product page show exactly how it changes.

### Can I reorder the same design later?
Yes. You can place a new order with the same artwork whenever you need more, for example when new members join.

### Can I add names or numbers for a team?
You can add text in the designer. Each order uses one design per product, so for different names on each piece you would place separate orders.

### How do I pay and how is it delivered?
You pay by card at checkout, and items are delivered to a Singapore address. Delivery is S$9.90 flat per order, or free over S$80.
