For a Singapore office, choose a mug for the pantry, a tumbler for the commute and a bottle for desks and meetings, then print a simple, readable design on each. Drinkware is used every day, so it keeps your name or logo in front of people far longer than most giveaways.

## Why drinkware works in Singapore workplaces

Singapore's climate means people drink water constantly, and many carry a bottle or tumbler from home to the office. Bring-your-own-cup habits are also common at coffee shops and event booths, where bringing a reusable cup is a familiar idea. A custom [mug, tumbler or bottle](/products?category=c-51baa713) fits naturally into that routine, and it is less likely to be forgotten in a drawer than a novelty item.

## Mug, tumbler or bottle: how to choose

### Mugs
Best for the pantry and the desk. Mugs suit hot drinks, and they are a classic choice for staff welcome kits and festive gifts. They stay put in the office, so the design is seen by colleagues and visitors.

### Tumblers
Best for people on the move. A tumbler with a lid suits the commute, takeaway coffee and BYO-cup discounts. If your staff are always walking between meetings and the lift lobby, this is the one.

### Bottles
Best for all-day hydration, gym bags and outdoor events. Bottles suit sports days, community runs and CCA activities.

Always read the product page for capacity, material and care instructions, since these vary by item.

## Designing the print

Drinkware has a curved surface, so a design that looks balanced on a flat screen can sit differently on the product.

- **Keep it simple:** a logo, a short name or a single motif reads well on a curve.
- **Use the live mockup:** the designer shows the product from several angles, so check the wrap-around view.
- **Design each print side separately** where the product allows it.
- **Choose artwork carefully:** 300 dpi is recommended and 150 dpi is the minimum, with transparent PNG or vector for logos.
- **Expect slight colour shifts:** screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result.

Most products are printed full-colour, so a gradient or a photo is fine and does not change the price. See the [ordering guide](/guide) for print method details.
