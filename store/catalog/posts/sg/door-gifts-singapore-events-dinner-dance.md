Good door gifts in Singapore are small, practical and clearly tied to the event: a printed tote bag, a reusable bottle or a desk accessory that guests will use again rather than leave on the table. Order about a month ahead, count carefully, and keep the design simple enough to read at a glance.

## What makes a door gift worth keeping

Anyone who has left a dinner with a bag of leaflets knows what ends up in the bin. Guests keep items that are useful, attractive and not overly branded. Ask yourself three questions:

- Will someone use this at home or at work within a week?
- Is it easy to carry out of a ballroom or seminar hall?
- Does the design look good without a huge logo?

If the answer to all three is yes, you have a keeper.

## Setting a budget per guest

Door gift budgets in Singapore tend to be small relative to the overall event spend, so work out your total first and divide by headcount. Remember to include delivery: a flat S$15 per order, free over S$150.

Because unit prices drop at higher quantities, the price tiers on each product page (such as 1+, 5+, 15+, 50+ or 100+) can help you decide whether to order slightly more. A modest buffer of extra pieces is sensible for late RSVPs and organisers.

## Designing the print

A clean design usually works best.

- **Keep text short:** an event name, year and perhaps a thank-you line.
- **Use good artwork:** 300 dpi recommended, and 150 dpi is the minimum. The designer warns you if resolution is too low.
- **Use a transparent PNG or vector for logos** so edges stay sharp.
- **Mind the colours:** screens can differ slightly from the printed result, so avoid relying on tiny shade differences.
- **Design each side separately** where the product allows it, for instance a logo on the front and the date on the back.
