Sports clubs, community groups and schools can raise money with custom merchandise by choosing one or two simple items, ordering only what members have pledged for, and pricing each item above its cost. Because every product can be ordered from one piece with no setup fee, you do not need to fund a large run up front or store boxes of unsold stock.

## Why small runs suit community fundraising

Traditional merchandise often means a big minimum order, a deposit and a garage full of leftovers. Volunteer treasurers rarely want that risk.

With a no-minimum model you can:

- Open a pre-order list and only buy what people have committed to.
- Run a second batch later for late joiners.
- Test a design with a single piece before telling the whole club.

Unit price drops at higher quantities (tiers such as 5+, 15+, 50+ are shown on each product page), so a bigger combined order costs less per item. Printing is included in the unit price.

## Fundraising plan in five steps

This is a simple method any committee can follow:

1. **Pick one hero item.** One well-chosen item is easier to promote than six.
2. **Design once.** Upload your crest or artwork in the online designer and check the live preview from several angles.
3. **Open pre-orders.** Collect names, sizes where relevant, and payment through your usual club channels, with a clear closing date.
4. **Place one combined order.** Use the price tiers to lower cost per item, and check whether you reach free delivery over A$99.
5. **Hand out and thank.** The order arrives at a single address, so arrange a pick-up day at training or the school office.

## How to price for a profit

Work from the total, not from guesswork.

- Add up the unit cost, your share of delivery, and any spare pieces you want for prizes or the committee.
- Divide by the number of items you expect to sell.
- Add your fundraising margin on top and round to a friendly price.

Delivery is a flat A$9.99 per order, and free on orders over A$99, so a single order is better than lots of small ones. Do not forget to include delivery in your costing if you stay under that line.

## Design tips that keep clubs looking sharp

- **Use your crest as a transparent PNG or vector file.** Recommended resolution is 300 dpi, with 150 dpi as the minimum.
- **Print full-colour.** Most products are printed all over using transfer or dye-sublimation, so gradients, photos and team colours are all fine and colour count does not change the price.
- **Use each print side.** Put the crest on the front and sponsor logos or a season year on the back, since each side is designed separately.
- **Allow for colour variation.** Screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result, so avoid designs that depend on one exact shade.

## Timing your fundraiser

Production starts after payment and commonly takes around 5 working days, then delivery takes about 8 to 14 working days after dispatch. That is roughly four weeks end to end, so build in some slack.

- Working back from a grand final, presentation night or school fete, order at least five weeks beforehand.
- Do not promise members a delivery date until the order has actually been dispatched.
- If you are only a little unsure, order one piece first.

## FAQ

### Is there a minimum order for club merchandise?
No. Every product can be ordered from one piece, with no setup fee, so you can order exactly what your members have paid for.

### Do we have to pay a deposit or set-up cost for our logo?
No. Printing is included in the unit price and there is no setup fee on these products. You pay by card through Stripe checkout when you place the order.

### Can the club get a discount code?
No discount codes are offered. The way to lower the unit price is to order a larger quantity, using the tiers shown on each product page.

### Can items be sent to each member's home?
No. Each order goes to one address in Australia, so most clubs have it delivered to a committee member or the school office and hand items out from there.

### What if an item arrives faulty or misprinted?
Report it within 7 days of delivery with photos and it will be remade or refunded free of charge. Items are made to order, so there are no returns for change of mind. See the [returns policy](/returns).
