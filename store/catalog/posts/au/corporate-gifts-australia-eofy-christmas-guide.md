The best way to plan corporate gifts in Australia is to work backwards from the date your team or clients need them, set a per-head budget first, and order about five weeks ahead. The three big pressure points are end of financial year (EOFY) in June, Christmas, and the summer shutdown that follows. Custom-printed gifts that arrive early beat expensive ones that arrive in January.

## The Australian corporate gifting calendar

Most Australian businesses give gifts at the same few moments, so lead times get tight all at once.

- **End of financial year (June):** thank-you gifts for clients, suppliers and staff after a busy quarter.
- **Christmas and the summer shutdown (December to January):** the largest gifting window, with many offices closing for one to three weeks.
- **Work anniversaries and milestones:** spread across the year, so there is no deadline crunch.
- **Onboarding:** welcome packs for new starters, ordered a piece at a time.

Because the shutdown starts before Christmas for many workplaces, your real deadline is often mid-December, not 25 December.

## Set a budget per head

Decide the budget before you browse. A simple structure keeps decisions fast and fair:

- **Light touch (low budget):** a printed mug, pouch or mouse pad for every staff member or a large client list.
- **Mid-range:** a tote, bottle, blanket or cushion with a name or logo, for key clients and long-serving staff.
- **Premium:** a larger home or living item for top clients, or a combination of two items for senior milestones.

Remember to add delivery to the budget. Delivery is a flat A$20 per order, and free on orders over A$200, so one consolidated order is usually more economical than several small ones. Unit price also drops at higher quantities, with tiers shown on each product page, so a single design across the whole team often goes further.

## Lead times: how early to order

Here is the sequence every order follows:

1. You design online and pay by card through Stripe checkout.
2. Production starts after payment, commonly around 5 working days (the exact time is on each product page).
3. The order is dispatched, then takes about 8 to 14 working days to reach your Australian address.

Add those together and allow at least four to five weeks, more if you need time to approve artwork or to gather names from staff. Delivery dates cannot be promised, so build in a buffer. For a mid-December shutdown, aim to order in early to mid November at the latest. For EOFY gifts, order in early May.

## Quick checklist: corporate gift order in six steps

Use this list to run any corporate gift order from start to finish:

1. Choose the date people must have the gift, then subtract five weeks.
2. Set a per-head budget and a headcount.
3. Pick one product type and one design for the whole group.
4. Upload a transparent PNG or vector logo (300 dpi recommended, 150 dpi minimum) and check the live mockup from every angle.
5. Order one piece first if you want to see the real product before committing to a big run.
6. Place the full order in one go to take advantage of price tiers and free delivery over A$200.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Leaving artwork until the last week.** Low-resolution logos are the most common delay; the designer warns you when resolution is low.
- **Expecting exact colour matching.** Screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result, so avoid designs that depend on one precise shade.
- **Assuming individual delivery.** An order ships to one address, so plan how you will hand gifts out or forward them to remote staff.
- **Forgetting that items are made to order.** There are no returns for change of mind, though faulty or misprinted items are remade or refunded if reported within 7 days of delivery with photos. See the [returns policy](/returns).

## FAQ

### How far ahead should I order corporate gifts for Christmas in Australia?
Allow four to five weeks from the day you pay, covering production of around 5 working days plus about 8 to 14 working days of delivery. For a mid-December shutdown, that means ordering in early to mid November.

### Is there a minimum order for corporate gifts?
No. Every product can be ordered from one piece, and the unit price falls as quantity rises. Many businesses order a single item first to check quality.

### How much does delivery cost for a bulk order?
Delivery is a flat A$20 per order, and free on orders over A$200. Delivery is to Australian addresses only.

### Can I send gifts directly to each staff member?
No, each order goes to a single delivery address. You can either distribute the gifts yourself or place separate orders for each address.

### Can we get gift wrapping or a discount code?
Neither is offered. Printing is included in the unit price, and the price tiers on each product page are the way to save on larger quantities.
