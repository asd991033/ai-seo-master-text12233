## Designing for a car or outdoor product

Printing is mostly all-over and full-colour, so photos, gradients and patterns are fine. A few points help the result look sharp:

- **Use large, simple shapes.** A sun shade is seen from outside the car, so bold artwork reads better than fine detail.
- **Use a high-resolution file.** 300 dpi is recommended and 150 dpi is the minimum; the designer warns if your image is too low.
- **Keep logos as a transparent PNG or vector** so there is no white box around them.
- **Preview the mockup from several angles** before you order, and design each print side separately where the product has more than one.
- **Allow for small colour shifts.** Screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result.

## Planning a road-trip gift set

Here is a simple way to put together a theme without overspending:

1. Pick a main item, such as a sun shade or picnic rug.
2. Add one or two small companions, like a bottle and a pouch.
3. Use one design across all items so they look like a set.
4. Place everything in a single order so it ships together to one address.

Delivery is a flat A$9.99 per order, and free on orders over A$99, so a combined order of several items is usually more economical than separate ones. Note that gift wrapping is not offered, so plan how you will present the gift yourself.
