For a family day or team building event, order custom T-shirts by collecting sizes early, choosing one bold all-over or front design, and placing your order at least five to six weeks before the day. Because there is no minimum order, you can order the exact count with a few spares.

## Start with the event, not the shirt

Before you open the designer, settle three questions: who is wearing it, what will they be doing, and when is the event? A family day with children and grandparents needs a different approach from a corporate run or a school club camp.

Think about:

- **Activity.** Outdoor games in Malaysian heat call for a breathable, easy-to-wear design.
- **Age range.** Include [kids' clothing](/products?category=c-3fb228fe) if families attend.
- **Reuse.** Will people wear the shirt again? A simple design is more likely to be worn after the event.

## How to collect sizes without the chaos

Wrong sizes are the most common headache for event organisers. A little structure saves a lot of stress.

### Step-by-step size collection

1. Create a simple shared form or sheet with name, department or family, and size.
2. Share the size chart shown on the product page so people choose accurately.
3. Ask people to pick the size of a shirt they already wear and like, not their guess.
4. Set a firm deadline at least two weeks before you plan to order.
5. Add a few spares in popular sizes, since late joiners always appear.
6. Double-check the total before checkout.

For families, collect sizes per person, including children, and note the age group. For clubs and teams, collect sizes at a meeting rather than by chat message to reduce missed replies.

## Design tips for all-over print

Most products here are printed all-over and in full colour by transfer or dye-sublimation. That gives you more creative freedom than a small chest logo, but a few habits help:

- **Keep one focal point.** A strong central graphic with a calm background reads better from a distance than a busy collage.
- **Use gradients and photos freely.** Colour count does not change the price, so go for rich colour if it suits your theme.
- **Mind the seams and edges.** Check the live mockup from several angles so important text does not fall on a fold or edge.
- **Design each print side separately.** Put the event name on the front and a team number or slogan on the back.
- **Choose legible text.** Use bold, simple fonts at a large size so names and dates can be read at a glance.
- **Use good resolution.** Upload 300 dpi artwork where you can, with 150 dpi as the minimum. Use a transparent PNG or vector for logos.

Screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result, so avoid designs that depend on an exact shade match.

## A quick checklist for ordering event T-shirts

Use this list before you pay:

- Final headcount plus a few spares
- Sizes confirmed per person
- Logo file as transparent PNG or vector
- Event name and date spelled correctly
- Mockup checked from all angles
- Delivery address confirmed, with someone available to receive the parcel
- Order placed at least five to six weeks before the event

On budget, unit price falls as quantity rises, with tiers such as 1+, 5+, 15+, 50+, 100+ and 300+. Printing is included, with no setup fee. Delivery is a flat RM22 per order and free on orders over RM200, so a group order often qualifies.

Because each order goes to one address, plan to receive everything in one place, then hand shirts out at the office, school or venue.
