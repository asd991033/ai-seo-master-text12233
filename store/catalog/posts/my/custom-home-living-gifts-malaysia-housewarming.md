## Why home textiles make good gifts

A housewarming gift should suit a new home without taking up too much room. Textiles do that well. They are soft, practical and easy to personalise, and they work for visits during Hari Raya, Chinese New Year and Deepavali, when families tidy and refresh their homes.

The [Home and Living category](/products?category=c-229a0246) covers blankets, cushions, tablecloths, towels and kitchen textiles, so you can pick what suits the recipient.

## What to give for each occasion

### Housewarming

New homeowners often need small comforts. A printed cushion with the family name or a favourite photo adds personality to a sofa. A throw blanket suits air-conditioned living rooms, which many Malaysian homes have.

### Festive visits

During open house season, a tablecloth printed with a tasteful pattern can be a gift that gets used for the very next gathering. Kitchen textiles and towels also suit hosts who are preparing food for guests.

### Weddings and anniversaries

A blanket or cushion printed with the couple's names and date becomes a keepsake. For parents or grandparents, a photo-print blanket featuring family members is often a lasting favourite.

### Corporate and client gifts

A cushion or blanket in your brand colours, with a subtle logo, can suit clients who work from home. Keep the message light and the design clean.

## How to choose the right item

A quick guide:

- **Cushions:** best for small spaces and quick gifting.
- **Blankets:** best for photo prints and sentimental gifts.
- **Towels:** practical and universal, good for housewarmings.
- **Tablecloths:** best for festive hosts and family gatherings.
- **Kitchen textiles:** small, useful extras for those who love to cook.

Before ordering, think about the recipient's space. A bright, bold print suits a modern flat, while softer tones suit a traditional home.

## Design tips for home gifts

Most products are printed all-over in full colour by transfer or dye-sublimation, so you can use photos and gradients without extra cost. Colour count does not change the price.

1. **Choose a strong image.** One clear photo or pattern beats a crowded collage.
2. **Use high resolution.** Upload 300 dpi artwork where possible. The minimum is 150 dpi, and the designer warns you if resolution is low.
3. **Leave breathing space.** On large items such as blankets and tablecloths, avoid placing important text near the edges.
4. **Add a short message.** A name, date or one line is more elegant than a paragraph.
5. **Check the mockup.** The live preview shows the product from several angles, so look at it from each one.
6. **Remember colour differences.** Screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result.

## FAQ

Practical, personal items work best. A custom cushion, blanket or set of towels is useful from day one. Add a family name or a small message to make it personal.

Yes. Upload your photo in the online designer, scale it and check the mockup. Use 300 dpi where possible, with 150 dpi as the minimum.

### How early should I order a gift for Hari Raya or Chinese New Year?
Order four to six weeks ahead. Production takes around 5 working days after payment, and delivery takes about 7 to 12 working days after dispatch.

### Can I send the gift directly to the recipient?
Each order goes to a single Malaysia address, so you can enter the recipient's address for that order. You cannot split one order across several addresses.
