The best corporate gifts in Malaysia are useful, neutral and ordered early. Set a budget per head, pick one item that suits every race and religion on your list, add your logo, and order at least four to six weeks before the festival, because production and delivery together take time.

## Why timing matters more than the gift itself

Malaysia's festive calendar is crowded, and every celebration has its own gifting window. Custom-printed items are made to order, so they cannot be rushed at the last minute. Production usually takes around 5 working days after payment, and delivery takes about 7 to 12 working days after dispatch. That is roughly three weeks before you add time for artwork approval and for getting the gifts to your staff or clients.

The practical rule: work backwards from the day you want gifts in hand, then add a buffer. Festival dates shift each year, so check the calendar before you plan.

## A festive calendar for corporate gifting

### Hari Raya Aidilfitri

Gifts are usually given in the weeks before or after the fasting month. Choose items that suit any home: a [cushion or blanket](/products?category=c-229a0246), a [tote bag](/products?q=tote) for shopping, or a tumbler for the office. Avoid anything with imagery that may not suit the occasion, and keep designs warm and simple.

### Chinese New Year

Red and gold palettes are popular, but a custom design with your own brand colours also works. Think of gifts clients will actually use during the holidays, such as [Holiday and Seasonal items](/products?category=c-eaba8078) or a set of home textiles for visiting relatives.

### Deepavali

Bright, colourful artwork suits the Festival of Lights well. Because printing is full-colour, rich gradients and patterns will not cost extra, which is helpful for vibrant designs.

### Merdeka and Malaysia Day

These are ideal for staff appreciation and community events. A custom T-shirt or cap in national-colour-inspired artwork works well, and it can double as a gift for a company event or a family day.

### Year-end

This is the busiest window for client thank-you gifts and staff appreciation. Order early in the quarter, not in December.

## Budget per head: a simple framework

Prices vary by product and quantity, so check the price tiers on each product page. As a planning guide, many Malaysian companies divide recipients into tiers:

- **Staff and general clients:** a modest, practical item such as a mug, pouch or tote, ordered in larger quantities where unit prices drop.
- **Regular clients and partners:** a step up, such as a cushion, blanket or quality bottle.
- **Key accounts and senior contacts:** a small curated set of two or three items rather than one expensive piece.

Unit price falls at higher quantities, with tiers such as 1+, 5+, 15+, 50+, 100+ and 300+. Printing is included, and there is no setup fee, so you can order a different quantity for each tier.

## How to plan a corporate gift order: step by step

1. Fix your recipient list and divide it into tiers.
2. Set an RM budget per head for each tier, remembering the delivery fee.
3. Choose one product type per tier from the [product range](/products).
4. Upload your logo in the online designer and check the mockup from several angles.
5. Order at least four to six weeks before the festival.
6. Receive delivery at one address and distribute from there.

Delivery is a flat RM39 per order and free on orders over RM400, so consolidating your tiers into a single order can make sense.

## Design tips for company gifts

- **Use a transparent PNG or vector logo.** Aim for 300 dpi, with 150 dpi as the minimum. The designer warns you if resolution is low.
- **Keep it subtle.** A small logo and a clean message feel more thoughtful than a billboard.
- **Check colours.** Screen colours can differ slightly from the printed result, so avoid relying on an exact shade match for brand colours.
- **Order one first.** There is no minimum, so ordering a single piece is the simplest way to see the real product before committing to a bigger run.

## Things to avoid

- Leaving it until the last fortnight. Dated events need early orders.
- Planning to ship to many separate addresses. Each order goes to one delivery address, so gifts are delivered to you and you distribute them.
- Assuming you can cancel after production starts. Orders can be cancelled for a full refund only before production starts.
