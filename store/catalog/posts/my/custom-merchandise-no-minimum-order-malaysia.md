You can order custom merchandise in Malaysia from just 1 piece, with printing included in the unit price and no setup fee. That makes it practical for small businesses, online sellers and student clubs to test a design, then reorder in larger quantities when unit prices drop.

## Why no minimum order changes the game

Traditional merchandise often needs a big first order, which ties up cash before you know whether people want the product. A no-minimum model reverses this: start with one piece, see how it looks, then scale up.

This suits:

- **Small businesses** that want branded items for staff, clients or a shop counter.
- **Online sellers** who want to test a design before stocking more.
- **Student clubs and societies** with small, changing memberships and tight budgets.
- **Event organisers** who need a handful of items for committee members or guests of honour.

## How to start small: a five-step plan

1. **Pick one product.** Choose the item that fits your audience, not the whole catalogue.
2. **Design online.** Upload your logo or image, or add text, then drag, scale and rotate it in the designer.
3. **Check the mockup.** Review the live preview from several angles.
4. **Order 1 piece.** This is your sample. The store does not offer separate physical pre-production samples, so ordering a single piece is how you check quality.
5. **Scale up.** If you like the result, order more and benefit from lower unit prices.

## Understanding price tiers

Each product page shows price tiers, such as 1+, 5+, 15+, 50+, 100+ and 300+. The more you order of the same product, the lower the unit price. Printing is included, and there is no setup fee on these products.

A useful habit: before you order, look at the next tier up. If you plan to buy 12 and the 15+ tier is cheaper per piece, check whether the total works out better with three more. Always compare the total cost, including delivery.

Delivery is a flat RM22 per order, and free on orders over RM200. For small orders, combine several items into one checkout to avoid paying delivery twice.

## Tips by type of user

### Online sellers

- **Test before stocking.** Order one piece of each design, look at it in your hands, and photograph it for your own product page.
- **Keep designs simple.** Clean artwork reads better in small online photos.
- **Plan lead time.** Production is usually around 5 working days after payment, and delivery takes about 7 to 12 working days after dispatch. Build that into your launch date.
- **Check the delivery address.** Each order ships to one address, so it is a bulk delivery to you, not to your individual customers.

### Student clubs and societies

- **Agree a design in advance.** Share the mockup with the committee before ordering.
- **Order early for events.** Dated events like orientation week or a society fair need orders weeks ahead.
- **Use a shared card.** Payment is by card through Stripe checkout, so decide who will pay and reimburse them.
- **Add members later.** Since there is no minimum, you can reorder for new members without a big commitment.

## Artwork and limits

### Artwork guidelines in short

- Use 300 dpi artwork where possible; 150 dpi is the minimum.
- Use a transparent PNG or vector file for logos.
- Expect the designer to warn you if resolution is low.
- Allow for slight colour differences between screen and print.

The [ordering guide](/guide) covers artwork and print methods in more detail.

### What is not offered

To keep expectations clear, the store does not offer embroidery, engraving on these products, gift wrapping or sending to several different addresses in one order. If you need those, plan to handle them separately.

Yes. Every product can be ordered from 1 piece, with no minimum order and no setup fee on these products.

Printing is included in the unit price, and the price drops as quantity rises across tiers such as 1+, 5+, 15+, 50+, 100+ and 300+. The tiers are shown on each product page.

### How long does it take to get my order?
Production is commonly around 5 working days after payment. Delivery takes about 7 to 12 working days after dispatch.

### What does delivery cost?
Delivery is a flat RM22 per order, and it is free on orders over RM200. Orders ship to Malaysia addresses.

### What if I change my mind after ordering?
Items are made to order, so there are no returns for change of mind. You can cancel for a full refund before production starts. Faulty, misprinted, wrong or transit-damaged items are remade or refunded within 7 days of delivery with photos. See the [returns policy](/returns).
