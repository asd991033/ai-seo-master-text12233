The best door gifts are small, useful and personal enough that guests keep them. For Malaysian weddings, kenduri and annual dinners, pick something people use daily, such as a pouch, tote bag, mug or small towel, add your names or logo, and order about six weeks before the event.

## What makes a good door gift

A door gift only works if it leaves the venue with the guest and stays in use. Think about what your guests will do the moment they get home: put it on a shelf, drop it in the bin, or use it next week.

Good door gifts share a few traits:

- **They are useful.** Everyday items outlast novelty ones.
- **They are light.** Guests carry them home after dinner, perhaps with children in tow.
- **They suit everyone.** Avoid designs or items that may not suit all faiths or age groups.
- **They look personal.** A name, date or small message turns an ordinary item into a keepsake.

## Door gift ideas by event

### Weddings and receptions

Guests at weddings range from grandparents to toddlers, so keep it universal. A small [pouch or tote](/products?category=c-a56b2fcd) printed with the couple's names and date is easy to carry and easy to reuse. A [mug or tumbler](/products?category=c-51baa713) also works well, as does a small kitchen textile for the home.

### Kenduri and family gatherings

For a kenduri, guests often take home food as well, so a practical bag is a thoughtful pairing. Choose neutral artwork with a short message of thanks. Keep colours soft and the text simple.

### Annual dinners and company events

Here the gift can carry your company branding. A tote, mug or [mouse pad or desk mat](/products?category=c-169b68e3) suits office-going guests. If there is a theme, print the theme artwork so the gift is a souvenir of that night.

### Birthdays, aqiqah and milestone parties

Smaller guest lists mean you can go a little more personal. Since there is no minimum order, you can order exactly as many as you have guests. A cushion cover or small blanket makes a memorable gift for close family.

## Door gift design checklist

Use this as a quick reference when preparing your artwork:

- Use a transparent PNG or vector file for logos and monograms.
- Upload images at 300 dpi where possible; 150 dpi is the minimum.
- Add the names, date and a short thank-you line in the online designer.
- Check the live mockup from several angles before ordering.
- Remember that each print side can be designed separately, so use the back for a short message or a date.
- Allow for small colour differences between your screen and the printed result.

## Timeline: when to order

Door gifts have a fixed deadline: the day of the event. Production usually takes around 5 working days after payment, and delivery takes about 7 to 12 working days after dispatch.

A safe plan for an event date:

- **Six weeks before:** finalise the design and order one piece to check it.

Avoid leaving the order to the final fortnight, since delays can happen and dated events cannot move.

### Packing and handing out

The store does not offer gift wrapping, so plan packing yourself. Many hosts use simple paper bags, ribbons or a small card. Your order is delivered to a single address, so choose someone reliable to receive it, and keep the pieces together until the event.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Choosing something fragile that is hard to carry home.
- Printing a date that later changes.
- Ordering exactly the guest count with no spares.
- Waiting for a physical sample. The easiest sample is ordering 1 piece.
